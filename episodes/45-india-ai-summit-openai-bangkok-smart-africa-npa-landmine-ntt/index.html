🔥 Top Stories

India AI Impact Summit Declaration — 91 Nations Sign

The broadest multilateral AI governance consensus to date. Delhi reframed AI summits from risk to impact, creating space for the US and China to sign the same document. The key output: the Framework for the Trusted AI Commons — a living platform for shared AI resources, not another declaration. Bletchley got 29, Seoul got 11, Paris got 60. Delhi got 91.

OpenAI AI Jam for Disaster Management — Bangkok

50 disaster management professionals from 12 Asia-Pacific countries gathered with Gates Foundation, Asian Disaster Preparedness Center, and DataKind to build custom GPTs and workflows for situation reporting, needs assessment, and public comms. Key data: ChatGPT saw a 17× spike in cyclone queries during Ditwah in Sri Lanka. AI is being used informally in crises whether organizations deploy it or not.

Smart Africa + Wadhwani AI — Continental AI DPG Platform

42 African nations and Wadhwani AI signed an MOU to build a continental AI Digital Public Goods repository — tested, adaptable tools for climate, agriculture, healthcare, and disaster management. Responsible sequencing model: govern first, deploy second. Target: five countries live within 12 months. Kenya's Africa-first Open Source Programme Office is already operational.

Norwegian People's Aid + MRead — Magnetic Resonance Landmine Detection

New technology detects explosive molecular signatures directly in soil, bypassing metal casings entirely. Field-validated in Angola. Eliminates 100+ false signal investigations per live mine found with conventional detectors. Direct impact: faster land return to civilian communities post-conflict.

NTT/DOCOMO — Predictive 5G Network Slicing for Disaster Response

Verified technology enabling advance prediction of network slice stability under fluctuating conditions before field deployment. Eliminates the failure mode of discovering communications can't handle disaster zone conditions after teams are already positioned.
