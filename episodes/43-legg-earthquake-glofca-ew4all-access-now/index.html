🔥 Top Stories

LEGG: AI Generates Street-Level Earthquake Damage Views from Aerial Imagery

Researchers from Ohio State, Stanford, and UT Austin unveiled LEGG — LoRA-Enhanced Ground-view Generation — which synthesizes 3D street-level views of earthquake damage from aerial drone imagery alone. Validated against the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquake, it fine-tunes on just 3,000 samples. Directly addresses the critical gap in the 72-hour search-and-rescue window when only aerial overflights are available.

GLOFCA Deploys AI Glacial Flood Early Warning in Tajikistan

The UN-led GLOFCA project deployed AI integrated with local sensor networks in Tajikistan to predict glacier melt rates and map hazard zones weeks in advance. The Fedchenko Glacier — Central Asia's largest — feeds glacial lakes that can rupture catastrophically. Downstream risk spans Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and Afghanistan. Supported by the Adaptation Fund.

EW4All Financial Tracking AI-Assistant (EACL 2026)

A team working with the World Meteorological Organization presented an open-source AI agent that automates tracking of climate finance flows for Early Warning for All initiatives. Using RAG and chain-of-thought reasoning over MDB documents, it achieves 87% accuracy, 89% precision, 83% recall. Published as an open-source Digital Public Good.

Access Now: Humanitarian AI Procurement in Crisis

Access Now published a report — based on 70+ interviews — finding the aid sector is experiencing "corporate capture" by cloud-first technology vendors. Cases documented where chatbots delivered misleading information to vulnerable users. The report calls for procurement frameworks accounting for algorithmic bias, cybersecurity risk, and informed consent before signing AI vendor agreements. --- ## Governance Week in Review Three major frameworks in three days: SAFE AI (CDAC/Alan Turing, Thursday), WHO AI Mental Health Consortium (earlier in week), Access Now procurement report (today). Organizations with AI procurement decisions pending have significant new guidance to apply. ---
